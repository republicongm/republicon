FAQ

Does the game keep going while it's closed? Yes: your country earns income without you (90% of normal for the first 4 hours, less after that), and when you return we show you how much has come in. Setting the clock forward does not speed up the game.

How do I start over? Study → “Resign and Start Over.”

Where is my save? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

Do I need an internet connection? No. If you are online, the game only checks the exact time (details in the privacy policy).

How do I turn off reminders? Game settings (the gear at the top) → “Reminders,” or iPhone Settings → Notifications → Republicon.

Are there any real-money purchases? No. All currency is in-game.

Is this about a real country? No. All countries, people, parties and events are fictional. This is satire.
