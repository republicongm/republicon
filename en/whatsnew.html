What’s New

Version 1.0 · November 3, 2026

First release

You run a made-up country: build cities, argue with the Seym, keep the economy afloat and win elections. Here is what the game has from day one.

  • Living isometric cities: 15 cities across 5 regions, with roads, rails, stations and airports.
  • An economy of levers: taxes, the key rate, the budget and money printing. Every lever explains itself and shows an honest forecast.
  • A team of ministers, agencies with staff and equipment, reforms and bills through the Seym.
  • Every city has its own mayor, with their own personality, requests and hard choices.
  • The world around you: trade contracts, treaties, neighbors, the army and Projects of the Century.
  • Elections once a week in real time: approval depends on how people actually live.
  • Football: a stadium in your city, your own club and the Concord League.
  • The president’s personal affairs: bank, company, stock exchange, property, cars and wardrobe.
  • The president’s phone: StateFeed, news and messages from ministers and mayors.
  • Every ministry has its own live figures, and schools and universities train staff for factories and hospitals.
  • Ministers come to you with ideas, and the Sejm has six factions whose votes you win with concessions.
  • A Rainy Day Fund, the pump price and the calm Liberto Basket fund on the stock market.
  • A Morning Brief from the Prime Minister and a breakdown of what happened while you were away.
  • Up to three countries on one device, plus a Crisis Trainer.
  • A Term Promise, a chapter about you in the school textbook, and a stadium and station named after you.
  • People move between cities, and your cars drive through the streets in a motorcade.
  • A Trust Ladder with every country and launches from the spaceport.
  • Play in Russian, Ukrainian, English or Polish, on iPhone and iPad.
